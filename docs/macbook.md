### Mac Account Blocked? Unable to login?

If you're locked out of your Mac, you can get your recovery key in one of two ways:

- **Company Portal (Managed Macs):** Open **Company Portal** > select your device > **Get Recovery Key**.
- **MyIT:** Go to **MyIT > My Services > Intune Managed Mac** and request a recovery key. Manager approval is required.

Once you have the recovery key:

1. Start your Mac in **Recovery Mode**.
1. Select **Forgot all passwords** and enter the recovery key.
1. Open **Terminal** and run `resetpassword`.
1. Set a new password and restart your Mac.

Further information can be found in a [KB article in MyIT](https://myit.siemens.com/myitportal?id=kb_article&sysparm_article=KB0321032&table=kb_knowledge&searchTerm=recovery%20key%20macOS).
